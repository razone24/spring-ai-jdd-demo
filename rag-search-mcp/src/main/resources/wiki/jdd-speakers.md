## Session: The Future of Software Architecture in the Age of AI

A walk through four iterations of an internal Q&A chatbot: the naive "whole wiki in the prompt" approach, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), RAG with a semantic cache and chat history, and finally an agentic orchestrator that reaches its tools over the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The talk closes with a live Spring AI demo running on a local LLM, with guardrails, bounded tool loops, conversation memory, audit, cost tracking and Grafana dashboards.
