# JDD 2026 — Venue & Practical Information (attendee FAQ)

> Demo data: practical details below are sample content for the conference assistant demo.

## Venue

Hotel Metropolo, ul. Orzechowa 11, Kraków. Session rooms: Room A (main hall, keynotes), Room B, Room C, Room D, and the JUG Lounge for community sessions. The expo area with partner booths is in the foyer between Room A and Room B.

## Getting there

- From Kraków Airport (KRK): the airport train to Kraków Główny takes about 20 minutes, then taxi or tram to the venue (about 15 minutes).
- From Kraków Główny railway station: about 15 minutes by tram or taxi.
- Parking: a limited number of paid underground spaces at the hotel; attendees are encouraged to use public transport.

## Registration and badges

Badge pick-up opens at 08:00 on day 1 and 08:30 on day 2 in the lobby. Bring the QR code from your Eventory ticket and a photo ID. The badge is required for all sessions, meals and the after-party. Lost badges can be reprinted at the registration desk.

## Wi-Fi

Network: **JDD2026** — password: **Java4Ever!**. A separate network **JDD-Speakers** is reserved for speakers and live demos.

## Food

Coffee is available all day in the foyer. Lunch is served 12:00–13:00 on both days in the restaurant on the ground floor. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available; declare other dietary needs at registration.

## After-party

Day 1 (Tuesday 20 October) from 19:30 at the Hotel Metropolo rooftop and bar. Entry with the conference badge; drinks and snacks included.

## Recordings and slides

Talks in rooms A–D are recorded. Recordings are published to ticket holders a few weeks after the conference and later on the JDD YouTube channel. Speakers share slides through the conference app.

## Code of conduct

JDD is a harassment-free event for everyone. Report any issue to staff wearing orange T-shirts or at the registration desk.
