# JDD 2026 — Overview

JDD (Java Developers Day) is a two-day software engineering conference in Kraków, Poland, with 20 years of community history. The 2026 edition runs on Tuesday 20 and Wednesday 21 October 2026 at Hotel Metropolo, ul. Orzechowa 11, Kraków.

- Audience: around 600 engineers
- 50+ speakers and 60+ sessions over two days
- 30+ technology partners in the expo area
- Organiser: PROIDEA, organiser of tech conferences and hackathons in Poland and CEE for 20+ years
- Website: jdd.org.pl

## Tracks

Backend & Java/JVM · Architecture & Distributed Systems · Cloud, DevOps & Platform Engineering · Software Quality, Testing & Reliability · Security · Platform · AI-Augmented Engineering · Future Software Engineering & Engineering Leadership.

## Community programme

- **JUGmajster** — Java User Groups from Kraków, Warsaw, Poznań, Wrocław, Tricity and visiting CEE groups run community sessions in the JUG Lounge on day 1 afternoon.
- **Unconference** — open-space sessions proposed by attendees, day 1 from 17:00 in rooms B–D.
